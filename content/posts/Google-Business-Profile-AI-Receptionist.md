## What Google Business Profile Actually Does for You

Most business owners treat their GBP as a directory listing. Name, address, phone number, done.

In reality it's your highest-converting traffic source:

- **Calls come in with high intent** — people searching locally are ready to book, not just browsing
- **Reviews build instant trust** — a 4.8-star rating does the selling before the call even connects
- **"Near me" searches are growing** — mobile local searches have increased every year for the past decade
- **GBP calls are often after hours** — customers search when they have time, not when you're open

The problem is GBP delivers the lead right to your phone — and your phone is the weakest link.
